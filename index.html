Kennis HCM Core Capabilities

Key Features of Kennis HCM

Everything your HR department, managers, and employees need to run operations efficiently — from first day of onboarding to annual performance appraisals.

1. Employee Records & Org Chart

Maintain one single, verified digital record per employee. Digital CNIC/passport storage, emergency contacts, job descriptions, promotion timelines, and visual company org hierarchy.

  • Dynamic reporting lines & team views
  • Centralized document & contract storage
Zero duplicate entries

2. Attendance & Leave Management

Real-time sync with biometric fingerprint and facial devices, browser clock-ins, or GPS-enabled mobile punches. Multi-tier leave workflows for casual, annual, sick, and maternity leaves.

  • Shift rosters, rotational schedules & overtime
  • Auto grace periods & late-mark deductions
The timesheet writes itself

3. Pakistan Statutory Payroll

Built specifically for Pakistan's corporate requirements. Pre-applied attendance, auto FBR Income Tax slabs calculation, EOBI contributions, Provident Fund, and 1-click bank transfer export files.

  • Automated FBR tax slabs & yearly reconciliation
  • Password-protected digital payslips for employees
Payroll signed off before dinner

4. Performance Management & OKRs

Align employee day-to-day work with company goals. Establish quarterly OKRs, KPI metrics, transparent 360-degree review cycles, and annual appraisals with clear merit-increase tracking.

  • Continuous manager-employee feedback notes
  • 9-box talent matrix & promotion succession
Clear merit-based growth

5. Automated Onboarding & Exit

Coordinate IT, HR, Finance, and Administration in one click. Send welcome kits, collect digital employee documents, allocate company laptops, and guarantee 100% asset recovery during offboarding.

  • Auto asset tagging (laptops, SIMs, fuel cards)
  • Clear final settlement & experience letters
No lost equipment or unreturned assets

6. Employee Self-Service (ESS) Portal

Empower your workforce with a mobile-ready self-service portal. Staff can review remaining leave balances, view confidential payslips, submit reimbursement claims, and request official certificates.

  • Web & Mobile responsive access
  • Reduces 80% of routine HR desk queries
Modern employee experience
